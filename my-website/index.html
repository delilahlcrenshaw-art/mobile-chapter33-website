Welcome to the official website of Maj Gen J. Gary Cooper - Mobile Chapter #33 of the Montford Point Marines Association.

Our Mission

Our mission is to honor the legacy of the Montford Point Marines by promoting leadership, community service, and mentorship among veterans and youth. We strive to preserve the history and contributions of our Marines while serving the Mobile community.
